# Credits

ComboShip Ultimate is an unofficial fan build. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by any project listed
here, and nearly all of the work in it is theirs.

## Lineage

**ComboShip Ultimate** is built from
[Varuuna's ComboShip](https://github.com/Varuuna/ComboShip), with
[junkx420's ComboShip co-op work](https://github.com/junkx420/ComboShip) and the cross-game forms, masks and items of
[Captain-Backwash's WomboComboShip](https://github.com/Captain-Backwash/WomboComboShip) (main, commit `35ae4d55`).
All of it sits on Ship of Harkinian and 2 Ship 2 Harkinian.

## Projects and people

- **ComboShip** by [Varuuna](https://github.com/Varuuna) and contributors: the cross-game randomizer everything
  here runs on.
- **ComboShip co-op fork** by [junkx420](https://github.com/junkx420/ComboShip): background world sync, the live
  partner feed and the game-switch notices (as WomboComboShip's README credits it).
- **WomboComboShip** by [Captain-Backwash](https://github.com/Captain-Backwash/WomboComboShip): Majora's Mask forms,
  masks and items in Hyrule, Ocarina of Time items in Termina, and the merged pause menus. The exact split of
  authorship between WomboComboShip and junkx420's fork has not been checked.
- **Ship of Harkinian** by the [HarbourMasters](https://github.com/HarbourMasters/Shipwright) team, including the
  Anchor co-op mode.
- **2 Ship 2 Harkinian** by the [2 Ship 2 Harkinian](https://github.com/2ship2harkinian/2ship2harkinian) team.
- **libultraship** by [Kenix3](https://github.com/Kenix3/libultraship) and contributors.
- Inspired by [OoTMM](https://ootmm.com/).
