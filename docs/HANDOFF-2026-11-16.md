## Done this session
- **Co-op enemy sync is on the main line**, in both games and for bosses:
  - Each enemy has an owner, who sends position, HP and status at ~10 Hz.
  - Deaths are shared, and the drop happens once.
- **No health limits.** HP × players (Shared > Settings > Network > Co-op Combat, up to +1500% per extra player) lives
  in an uncapped 32-bit pool:
  - The enemy's own HP field only shows the pool's fraction of its vanilla bar.
  - Phase thresholds fire at the same fraction.
  - Death happens exactly when the pool empties.
  - A boss that resets its own HP re-seeds the pool.
- **MM fix:** while dormant, MM's Anchor dropped the room roster. A player who crossed into Termina after the room
  filled saw no teammates, so sync and scaling never started there. The dormant pump now keeps the roster (roster
  only; no announce, no toast).

## Verified (2 headless clients, LOCAL relay `scripts/wombo/run2.sh`, ×4)
- **Deku Tree Skulltula:** 2 HP took 8 hits, and the kill reached the other client once.
- **Queen Gohma:** 10 HP took 40 hits. The native bar read 5/10 at hit 20 and there was no early death. The solo
  client rescaled 10 → 40 when the second joined.
- **Termina Field (MM):** both clients showed players 2 / ×4. A 1-HP enemy took 4 hits, and the kill reached the
  other client once.
- **Unit tests:** `combo/coop/tests/coop_tests.cpp` passes all 826 checks.
- **Test hooks:** `COMBO_DEBUG_COOP_TRACE`, `COMBO_DEBUG_COOP_HIT=<frame>[,<every>]`, and
  `gCombo.Coop.EnemyHpPercent` in the clients' comboship.json.
